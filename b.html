Our mission.

Instead of feeding the vicious cycle of news demonetization that hurts quality journalism the most, advertisers should kickstart a virtuous cycle of investing in news that allows brands to reach valuable audiences and gives quality news content the financial stability it needs to thrive.

Mark PennChairman & CEO, Stagwell

To debunk the assumptions that have pushed advertisers away from news, Stagwell launched the Future of News Initiative with a single goal: reinvigorating the relationship between news and marketing. Recent discourse around brand safety — the effort of protecting a brand’s reputation by controlling its proximity to damaging content — has disproportionately hurt the news industry, as advertisers back away from news out of misconception and an abundance of caution.

The evidence is unambiguous. Ads placed beside stories judged “not brand safe” performed on par with ads next to “brand safe” content, a finding now replicated across six markets with HarrisX. The initiative published its mission statement across half a dozen publications, bringing journalists, publishers and brand leaders into the same room to show that news is not a risk to be managed but a platform that delivers business results.
