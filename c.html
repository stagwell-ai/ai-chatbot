* The Future of News Initiative brings together a coalition of news media and marketing industry leaders for fulsome discussions about the opportunities for news media, rooted in groundbreaking research. Together, journalists, publishers and brand leaders identify opportunities for industry growth and partnership, showcasing news as an essential tool for driving successful business results. At Stagwell, we believe a thriving news industry is not only the foundation of a healthy democracy, but also a critical platform for marketers and advertisers.

Our mission — instead of feeding the vicious cycle of news demonetization that hurts quality journalism the most, advertisers should kickstart a virtuous cycle of investing in news that allows brands to reach valuable audiences and gives quality news content the financial stability it needs to thrive. — Mark Penn, Chairman & CEO, Stagwell
  2. The evidence is unambiguous: ads adjacent to stories judged “not brand safe” performed on par with ads next to “brand safe” content — a finding replicated across markets.
