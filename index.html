What is real and what is not

The Workspace and The Dashboard make genuine model calls on a handful of surfaces. Each one carries a receipt naming the model and its latency — if it is not badged, it is not live. Everything else is illustrative: every score, percentage and projection is derived from the domain, so a given company always reads the same way. The Catalogue and The Platform are scripted throughout.

Nothing is submitted or stored anywhere, on any of the four.
