SEEDANCE 2 PROMPTS — STAGWELL.AI MARKETING CLOUD CAROUSEL
One standalone block per company: a SETTINGS line for the generator and a
PROMPT to paste as-is. Target file names once rendered: /assets/img/<id>.mp4.

PRODUCTION NOTES (read once)
- Resolution, aspect ratio, and duration are generator SETTINGS in Seedance,
  not prompt text. Set them in the tool: 1080p, 16:9, 10 seconds. The prompts
  below contain no pixel specs on purpose.
- Looping: a text prompt cannot guarantee a seamless loop. Either use
  Seedance's first-frame/last-frame image conditioning with the same still,
  or plan a post fix (crossfade the final ~1 second) before the clips go on
  the site. The site plays them muted, autoplay, loop.
- Color: the prompts describe the palette in words (deep navy, teal, warm
  amber). Models don't match hex codes — final grade to brand values
  (navy #003349, teal #009CBD, amber #FFB81C) happens in post if needed.
- Prompts 2 and 8 depict interfaces, which is where video models most often
  hallucinate garbled text despite the negative instruction. Budget a few
  rerolls for those two and pick the cleanest take.
